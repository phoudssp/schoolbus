1 · Student approaches the bus

Noi walks up to her assigned bus at her home stop. Her SafeRide ID card is in her hand.

2 · Tap or scan

She offers her card to the reader. NFC tap or QR scan — both work. The driver sees her name on the tablet.

3 · Reader confirms

Green flash + beep. The boarding event is captured: student, bus, stop, GPS, timestamp.

4 · Event reaches the cloud

The driver's tablet sends the event to SafeRide. If there's no cell signal, it queues locally and syncs later.

5 · Parent + dispatcher notified

Mom gets a push notification on the parent app. The dispatcher's live manifest updates in real time.
